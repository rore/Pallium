It atomically spends the immutable endpoint/trial key before one fixed native owner call. The spent fence remains after revoke, cleanup, service restart, epoch/revision change, and a later delivery to that endpoint. No later ticket for the endpoint may authorize another owner action under this finite experiment. Never create a fake accepted native reservation or infer suppression from the one-second delay. Missing positive source exit within the finite 180-second window ends the trial without archive, forced exit or blind repeat.
