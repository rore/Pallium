## Corrected finite inventory authority

The separate operator policy has exact fields: `version=1`, `action="desktop-inventory-only"`, `enabled`, `revoked`, monotonic `revision`, UTC `expires_at` (no more than 300 seconds from arming), `service_pid`, `service_creation`, `service_epoch`, `source_pid`, `source_creation`, `desktop_pid`, `desktop_creation`, `desktop_user_sid`, `desktop_executable` and `desktop_version`. All identity values come from the independently verified operator baseline and OS-authenticated peers, not model parameters. Protected storage/CAS mechanics are reused; shadow-only policy semantics are unchanged.

Readiness carries no endpoint or current-turn identity and does not authorize transfer. At admission the service checks enabled/not revoked, exact current revision, finite expiry, its own process/epoch, the authenticated source PID/creation and the armed Desktop process/user/executable/version. The admitted same-channel transfer rechecks the same policy and peers before connection. The actual native pipe server must match the retained armed Desktop handle; that identity/version is checked again before each fixed inventory action. No inferred controller/turn is invented.

There is one pending readiness/admission/registration slot, not an unbounded table. Same authorized tuple/revision is idempotent; concurrent/different tuples are rejected or busy, not last-writer-wins. Explicit revocation/expiry and conclusive resource close are required before a new armed revision replaces it. Malformed/timeout responses discard usable custody; unresolved I/O remains owned/unavailable with no reconnect or replacement. Native inventory contents and endpoint capability are never published or persisted.
