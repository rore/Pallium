# Session History developer

Apply the shared [developer role](developer.md) together with this specialty. Existing session settings and assignments remain unchanged.

Own assigned Session History changes across capture fidelity, provenance, visibility and scope, search, expansion, and retention. This is guidance, not permission or a requirement for a separate session. Follow [repository instructions](../AGENTS.md) and [Session History documentation](../docs/session-history.md).

- Preserve source identity, provenance, and visibility through capture and retrieval. Use exact supplied scope and provider-returned references; never infer identity or access from a directory, recipient, or historical narrative.
- Treat retrieved history as evidence of prior work, not current state or new approval. Verify live ownership, service state, and delivery status before acting on historical claims.
- Attribute a turn to its validated start-of-turn Relay name. A later takeover or capture retry must not rewrite that historical attribution; a historical label does not identify the current holder.
- Preserve content and lookup lineage across expansion, pagination, and bounded retries. Follow the installed pallium-memory skill's replay procedure; avoid re-reading delivered pages or silently treating partial results as complete continuity.
- Apply both [retrieval and evaluation invariants](../docs/context/lessons.md): retrieval alone never updates accessibility or ranking state; every evaluation names candidate-recovery, injection-precision, or downstream-task-effect. Improved recovery is not evidence of improved downstream outcomes.
- Generalize incidents into capture, retrieval, scope, provenance, retention, or compatibility failures. Use anonymized fixtures and caller-visible lifecycle checks, including empty, Unicode, conflict, and permission boundaries where applicable.

Use [testing conventions](../docs/testing-conventions.md) for evidence and [operations](../docs/context/operations.md) for service diagnostics, including embedding-provider health when search returns too little. Keep optional derived memory separate from authoritative source history.

Report the caller-visible change, provenance/scope guarantees, measured result and its limits, and remaining uncertainty to the task owner. Keep temporary research and assignments in work records.
